## Parameter-space coverage ("the hypercube")

Show how much of the space of setting combinations the player has explored. A single "percent explored" number collapses toward zero as settings unlock, so coverage needs complementary views.

- **Fog on the bubble plot:** Unexplored areas stay dark and each run clears its area. Players choose which two settings form the axes. Fog density in a cleared area shows how well the hidden settings are covered there, so projections stay honest.
- **Context counts on the relationship board:** Each cell shows how many distinct starting conditions a relationship has been tested in, and how spread out they were. A rule confirmed in one region is weak; one confirmed across very different regions is strong. "Depends" can only be found by testing in a new context.
- **Parallel coordinates:** One vertical axis per setting, one line per run, showing every dimension at once. A candidate late observation mode and Insight unlock.
- **Trigger from locked relationships:** Locking relationships in the prediction area could interact with or trigger the parallel-coordinates view, for example highlighting the runs that tested each locked relationship. (Stephen's idea; details open.)
- **Teaching moment:** When a new setting unlocks, the fog returns, showing the curse of dimensionality and why scientists change one variable at a time.
- **Constraint:** Coverage must never point at interesting unexplored regions, since that would act as a preview. Any such pointer belongs only in the hint system, on request.
