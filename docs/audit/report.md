Bins: no change, up, down, peaked, dipped, threshold (one direction, but flat at one end of the grid), irregular (more than one change of direction). `depends` means the bin differs between contexts; the details follow each table. `untested` means the simulated player could not buy that level. Only in-run purchases respect unlocks and energy: on a first run cooling and braking cannot be bought and turbulence unlocks at ignition. A context is left out of a sweep that would overwrite the setting that defines it.

These bins are for checking text and writing explanations. They never grade a player's untested prediction.
